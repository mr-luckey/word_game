1. INTRODUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This Privacy Policy explains how Appwaretech ("we," "us," or "our") collects,
uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the mobile game
Word Glow ("the App," "the Game").

By downloading, installing, or using Word Glow, you agree to the practices
described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Word Glow is a word-search puzzle game with optional account features, in-app
purchases, advertisements, cloud save, achievements, daily rewards, and a
global leaderboard.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Information You Provide Directly
------------------------------------
When you create an account or use account-related features, we may collect:

  • Display name (player name)
  • Email address
  • Password (stored and processed securely by Firebase Authentication; we do
    not store your plain-text password on our servers)
  • Profile photo URL (if you sign in with Google and your Google account
    includes a profile picture)

Account creation and sign-in methods supported:
  • Email and password registration
  • Google Sign-In

Shop purchases require you to be signed in before completing a transaction.


2.2 Gameplay and App Usage Data
-------------------------------
Whether you play as a guest or a signed-in user, the App processes gameplay
data to provide the game experience. This may include:

  • Level progress (levels completed, stars earned, completion times)
  • Virtual coins balance
  • Hints used and in-game tool usage
  • Achievement unlock status and gameplay statistics (e.g., streaks,
    hint-free completions)
  • Daily challenge status (streak, last claim time, reward eligibility)
  • Destination/theme progress across game worlds
  • Selected app theme and visual preferences
  • Sound effects and background music on/off settings

Guest players: The above data is stored locally on your device only, unless
you later sign in and choose to sync progress to the cloud.


2.3 Automatically Collected / Device Data
-------------------------------------------
When you use the App, certain information may be collected automatically by
us or by third-party services integrated into the App:

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • General network/connectivity status (Internet access is required for ads,
    sign-in, cloud sync, shop, and leaderboard features)
  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data (via Google AdMob)
  • Purchase transaction records (via Google Play Billing; we receive product
    IDs and purchase confirmation, not your full payment card details)
  • Firebase authentication tokens and user IDs
  • Crash or performance data that may be collected by underlying platform
    or SDK providers

We do NOT intentionally collect:
  • Precise GPS/location data
  • Contacts, SMS, call logs, or microphone/camera recordings
  • Health, financial account numbers, or government ID numbers

The App requests INTERNET permission on Android. No location, camera,
microphone, contacts, or storage (external) permissions are required for core
gameplay.


2.4 Local Storage on Your Device
--------------------------------
The App stores data locally using:

  • SQLite database (game progress, coins, achievements, stats)
  • SharedPreferences / local key-value storage (settings, VIP status,
    remove-ads flag, daily challenge timestamps, auth cache)

This local data remains on your device until you clear app data, uninstall the
App, or overwrite it through gameplay or account sync.


2.5 Cloud Storage (Signed-In Users Only)
----------------------------------------
If you sign in, we sync certain data to Google Firebase Cloud Firestore under
your unique user ID (UID), including:

  • Display name and email address
  • Coin balance
  • Level progress records (level IDs, stars, completion times)
  • Unlocked achievements and gameplay statistics
  • Purchased product IDs (e.g., coin packs, Remove Ads, VIP Pass)
  • Remove Ads and VIP entitlement flags
  • Profile update timestamps

Leaderboard: If you are signed in, your display name and coin total may appear
on the in-app global leaderboard (top players by coins). Other players can see
your display name and coin count on the leaderboard; your email address is NOT
shown publicly on the leaderboard.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Game
  • Save and restore your progress (locally and, if signed in, in the cloud)
  • Authenticate your account and keep you signed in across sessions
  • Process in-app purchases and deliver purchased items (coins, Remove Ads,
    VIP Pass benefits)
  • Show personalized game state (profile, achievements, destinations, levels)
  • Display leaderboard rankings for signed-in users
  • Show advertisements (unless you have purchased Remove Ads or have an
    active VIP Pass that suppresses ads)
  • Improve stability, security, and user experience
  • Respond to support requests and enforce our terms
  • Comply with legal obligations

VIP Pass benefits (when active) may include: bonus coins on level completion,
extra hints per level, enhanced daily rewards, and ad-free experience.

Analytics: The App includes an analytics service layer that is currently
minimal/disabled in production builds. If we enable analytics in a future
update, this policy will be updated accordingly.

5. ADVERTISING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Word Glow displays ads through Google AdMob, including:

  • Banner ads (e.g., on level selection screens)
  • Interstitial ads (shown periodically after level completion)
  • Rewarded video ads (optional; e.g., to earn bonus coins)

You can reduce ad tracking through your Android device settings:
  Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization (wording may vary
  by device and Android version).

Purchasing "Remove Ads" or activating "VIP Pass" disables most or all
advertising within the App, subject to product terms.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. IN-APP PURCHASES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Shop offers digital goods including:

  • Coin packs (consumable)
  • Remove Ads (non-consumable)
  • VIP Pass (non-consumable / subscription-style product depending on Play
    Console configuration)
  • Starter Pack (consumable)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We store purchase records locally and,
if you are signed in, in your cloud profile to restore entitlements and sync
across devices.

Refunds are handled according to Google Play refund policies. Contact Google
Play support for billing disputes.

7. ACCOUNT TYPES: GUEST VS. SIGNED-IN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Guest Play:
  • You can play without creating an account.
  • Progress is saved on your device only.
  • Cloud backup, leaderboard, and shop purchases requiring login are limited
    until you sign in.

Signed-In Play:
  • Your local progress may be merged with existing cloud data upon login.
  • Progress, coins, achievements, and purchases can sync to Firestore.
  • You can appear on the global leaderboard.
  • Shop purchases are tied to your account for sync and support.
